Privacy Policy

VietAIVibes Uploader · Effective November 25, 2026

This privacy policy describes how the VietAIVibes Uploader app handles information. The app is a private tool used only by the owner of the YouTube channel @VietAIVibes to upload the owner’s own videos to that channel.

Who this app is for

The app is not offered to the public. It does not provide accounts for anyone other than the channel owner, and it does not collect information from other people.

YouTube Data API

The app uses the YouTube Data API with these scopes:

  • youtube.upload — to upload videos to the owner’s channel
  • youtube.readonly — to confirm the signed-in channel before an upload

Use of the YouTube Data API is also subject to the YouTube Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy.

Information we store

When the channel owner signs in with Google, the app receives OAuth tokens so it can upload on the owner’s behalf. Those tokens are stored privately on the owner’s machine. They are never shared, sold, or transferred to third parties.

The app does not collect, store, or sell personal data from other users.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent to vietaivibes@gmail.com.
